Who is Soenario Sastrowardoyo and what did he do for Indonesia?

Home About us Soenario Sastrowardoyo Johannes Leimana Muhammad Yamin

Soenario Sastrowardoyo played a key role in 1928 Sumpah Pemuda, where he served as a speaker. He was involved in the founding of Indonesia National Party (PNI) with Sukarno and founded Perguruan Rakyat.
