Who is Muhammad Yamin and what did he do for Indonesia?

Muhammad Yamin was born on August 24, 1903 in Sawahlunto, West Sumatra, a member of the Sumatra Bond. He is known as a respected writer, historian, cultural figure as well as politician, and legal expert. Yamin is one of the pioneers of modern Indonesian poetry and his name is immortalized as a National Hero.
