Johannes Leimena

Who is Johannes Leimena and what did he do for Indonesia?

Johannes Leimena was born in Ambon, Maluku, in 1905 and was a member of Jong Ambon who became a committee member of the Second Youth Congress. Known as a movement activist, J Leimena became the longest-serving minister during President Soekarno's administration, namely 20 years without interruption.
